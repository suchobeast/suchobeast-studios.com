Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to Suchobeast Studios, an independent studio founded by Suchobeast (Italy). You can reach us at suchobeast08@gmail.com.

What data we collect

Only what you send us through the contact form and the developer application form: your name, email address, message and, for applications, your role and portfolio link. We use it only to answer you and to evaluate applications.

What we do not do

The site has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not set its own cookies. We do not sell your data.

Third-party services

  • Web3Forms delivers the form messages to our email inbox.
  • Google Fonts loads the site typeface: Google may receive your IP address when a page loads.
  • Links to Roblox, Discord, YouTube, X (Twitter) and Fandom lead to third-party sites with their own privacy policies.

Why we can use your data

You send us your data voluntarily to get a reply or to apply, which is the basis (GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (f)) for processing it.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to answer your message or evaluate your application, then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to suchobeast08@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Minors

If you are a minor, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us personal data.

Changes

We may update this page from time to time; the date above shows the latest version.
