OUR STORY

How the studio
was born.

Suchobeast Studios was originally created in 2024 by Suchobeast, with a small but passionate development team.

What began as a small group of developers has grown into a much bigger studio working on both EXTINCTION and PIRATES ERA, with a rapidly growing team and community that helps shape every update.

Today our goal is to release fun, interactive and unique games that players want to come back to.
